Meet Your Mentor

Driven by Purpose, Resilience & Global Vision

Welcome to Shree Sai Overseas Education Mentor, your trusted partner in navigating the journey towards higher education abroad. Established in 2024 by dedicated founder and lead education counsellor Ekta Ghetia, we are committed to guiding students towards achieving their academic and career aspirations on an international stage.

Ekta Ma'am brings over 12 years of experience as a certified professional career counselor. Having spent more than ten years at Khartoum American School (Sudan), she guided international students through the competitive university admissions landscape. When the war in Sudan prompted displacement, she turned adversity into mission: founding Shree Sai Mentor to give young minds authentic, heartfelt direction.

Starting in Grade 9 allows students to build a genuine, non-rushed profile. Top domestic and international universities evaluate all four years of high school performance (Grades 9-12). Many prestigious universities in the UK, USA, and Europe allow students to waive the IELTS/TOEFL requirement if they scored above a certain percentage (typically 70% or 75%+) in high school English (CBSE, ISC, or State Boards), or if English was the medium of instruction.
